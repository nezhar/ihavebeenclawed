Reporting

What makes a useful report?

Publish it on your tool’s issue tracker, a community forum, or your own blog, then send us the link. Include:

  • the tool and version
  • what happened
  • the damage
  • what you learned

We cover systems, data, and money. Incidents involving human tragedy belong in the AI Incident Database.
